# SuprSkan scorecard (SPIDER)

How well each way of reading a lumbar disc agrees with an expert. Generated by `score.py` from
`work/eval/predictions.csv`; every number is defined in [METRICS_SPEC.md](METRICS_SPEC.md) and is
recomputed independently by `recompute.py` (log: `recompute.log`). Do not edit the tables by hand.

**TSS weights: permission pending.** Every method here finds discs with TotalSpineSeg's released
weights, which NeuroPoly has not yet cleared for commercial use (`LICENSING.md`). These are research
results: nothing that depends on these weights goes into a paid product until NeuroPoly agrees
in writing.

## What is measured

- **Data:** [SPIDER](https://zenodo.org/records/10159290) (van der Graaf, van Hooff, Buckens et al., "Lumbar spine segmentation in MR images: a dataset and a public benchmark", Scientific Data 11, 264 (2024), doi:10.1038/s41597-024-03090-w; data doi:10.5281/zenodo.10159290, CC BY 4.0): 218 patients with low back
  pain from four Dutch hospitals, sagittal T1/T2 lumbar MRI. **One** musculoskeletal radiologist
  (26 years of experience) graded every disc: that single reader is the "truth" here, so agreement
  with that one reader is the measure, not agreement with the truth. Healthy people are not in this data.
- **Split:** by patient, seed 20260930 (`split.json`): 174 development patients in 5 folds, 44
  held-out test patients, stratified by scanner maker and field strength. SPIDER's own
  train/validation column is ignored.
- **Discs scored:** each graded disc is matched to the TotalSpineSeg disc with the highest overlap
  (Dice at least 0.3) with SPIDER's disc mask; only discs at T12-L1 to L5-S1 count. A graded disc
  TotalSpineSeg did not find stays in n and counts as wrong for every method. Patients with no T2
  series are left out. When a T2's mask lacks a graded disc that SPIDER annotated on the same
  study's T2 SPACE or T1 series, that disc's mask is taken from there, resampled through world
  coordinates (`prepare.py`; which ones are listed in `work/eval/spider/<split>/prepare.json`).
  The data-accounting tables give every count.
- **Items:** Pfirrmann grade (exact, within one grade, quadratic weighted kappa); herniation,
  narrowing, bulging, spondylolisthesis, upper and lower endplate change, and any Modic change
  (sensitivity and specificity at thresholds fixed on development folds, AUROC where the method
  gives a score). Modic types 1 and 3 are too rare (4 and 7 discs in all of SPIDER) to score
  separately; their counts are listed.
- **Confidence intervals:** 95%, 2,000 bootstrap resamples of patients.

## Methods

- **rules**: today's SuprSkan measurements (`metrics.py`): disc-to-CSF signal ratio mapped to a
  Pfirrmann grade with four cut-points, and disc height divided by its front-to-back length for
  narrowing; thresholds fitted on the other four folds. It has no rule for the other items.
- **panel**: one AI reader (Claude Opus 5.5, run as a Claude Code sub-agent) shown the T2 and T1
  images with the discs marked, prompted with `READER_BRIEF.md` and a per-disc grading instruction
  (`panel_runner.py --backend agents`). It gives a Pfirrmann grade with a probability per grade
  and a probability per yes/no item; four cut-points on its mean grade and a threshold per item
  are fitted on 10 tuning patients (development fold 0), the way the other methods fit theirs on
  development data. Development rows are 30 other patients (folds 1-4) never used to tune it;
  run on a subset of patients, named in n.
- **grader**: SuprSkan's disc grader (`grader.py`, model card `pipeline/models/disc-grader.md`),
  trained from scratch on development folds only. Development rows are each fold's model on its
  own held-out fold.
- **medgemma**: MedGemma 1.5 4B (Google, run locally, `medgemma_runner.py`), zero-shot on the
  grader's disc crops (three T2 planes and the middle T1 plane): its next-token probabilities for
  a Pfirrmann digit and for Yes/No per item, temperature-calibrated on the other folds. Its raw
  answer was grade 3 for 922 of 1004 discs and grade 1 for the rest; calibrated, it says grade 3
  for every disc, so its kappa is 0.
- **combo**: the mean of the grader's and MedGemma's calibrated probabilities, the combination
  rule fixed before it was run. It had to beat the grader's development Pfirrmann kappa to enter
  the final evaluation; it did not (`medgemma_qualify.json`), so neither it nor MedGemma has test rows.

## Read this before quoting a number

- **Optimistic on SPIDER.** TotalSpineSeg was trained on SPIDER's images and masks, these
  patients included, so disc finding here is better than it will be on scans it never saw. Every
  method depends on it. The `spider_masks` rows locate discs with SPIDER's own masks instead, which
  shows how much of the error is the grading alone. Expect lower scores on new scans.
- **One reader, one population.** Agreement with a single radiologist on a back-pain cohort from
  one country. Other radiologists disagree with each other on these items too.
- **Not a diagnosis.** SuprSkan is not a medical device; these numbers describe agreement on a
  research dataset and nothing more.
