Scans we cover

X-ray, CT, MRI and mammogram.

Each kind of scan opens in the viewer, and each has open research models trained on public data.

  • X-ray

    The viewer opens single pictures and whole series, with zoom and brightness.

    Research models

    • Chest X-ray research model
    • Fracture research model (X-ray)
    • Lumbar spine X-ray corner reader

  • CT

    The viewer steps through every slice, with a brightness window and zoom. A 3D view shows the organs and bones as coloured shapes.

    Research models

    • CT organ outliner
    • Lung nodule research model (CT)

  • MRI

    Spine MRI slices open the same way, with zoom and brightness. Discs and vertebrae can be coloured by name.

    Research models

    • Disc grading research model (MRI)

  • Mammogram

    Opens the picture for zoom and brightness, like any other X-ray style picture.

    Research models

    • Mammogram research model

Privacy

Your scan stays on your device.

Nothing is uploaded when you open a scan. The pictures are read by your own browser and are gone when you close the tab. If you ever choose to donate a scan to help the research, you see everything first, and what could identify you is removed in your browser.
