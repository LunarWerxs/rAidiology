How it works

  1. 1

    Find your files

    Put the hospital's disc in a computer, or find the ZIP from the patient portal. A phone cannot read a disc.

  2. 2

    Choose them

    Pick the disc, folder or ZIP on the Open a scan page. Your browser reads the files where they are.

  3. 3

    Look through the pictures

    Pick a set, scroll through it, zoom in and change the brightness. Today it shows pictures only; it says nothing about what is in them.

Run it on your own computer

Download one ZIP, unzip it and double-click a file. The same scan opener runs from your own computer, even with no internet.

How to run it yourself

Your scan stays yours

  • The page reads the files on your own device and draws them on your screen. Nothing is sent unless you choose to donate a scan, and there is no account.
  • The page does not use or keep your name. It reads your date of birth on your device only, to check you are 18 or older if you donate.
  • No analytics, no cookies on the pages you use, nothing loaded from anybody else.
  • To check, turn off Wi-Fi after the page has loaded and open your scan. It still works. Some browsers word the permission prompt as "Upload files to this site?"; nothing is sent.
