Where the models fall short

  • CT organ outliner. Both kidney cysts score 0.0000 on the test scans. Vertebra C6, the gallbladder, the portal and splenic veins and the adrenal glands are the next weakest.
  • Disc grading research model (MRI). The three current versions have not been scored on held-out test patients; their results are development results.
  • Lung nodule research model (CT). All the nodule-finding numbers are development scores from the same 888 scans used to choose between designs; there is no untouched test.
  • Mammogram research model. Where no lesion outline exists the score is close to a coin toss: version 38 scores 0.5726 on those 1,002 pictures.

What the viewer does, and what it does not.

It does

  • Open CT, MRI and X-ray discs and ZIPs on this device
  • Let you scroll through slices, zoom and change the brightness
  • Show the 3D sample scan with organs and bones named

It does not

  • Upload your scan
  • Read your report
  • Tell you what is wrong
  • Replace your doctor

Install it as an app

Add rAidiology to your computer or phone from the browser. It then opens offline, with no account.

Your scan stays on your device.

Nothing is uploaded when you open a scan. The pictures are read by your own browser and are gone when you close the tab. If you ever choose to donate a scan to help the research, you see everything first, and what could identify you is removed in your browser.
