Open a scan

Open your own scan

Choose the disc or the ZIP your hospital gave you. Your browser opens it on this device. Nothing is uploaded, and there is no account.

Drop your scan folder or ZIP here or use a button below.

Your browser will ask whether this page may read the files. Allowing it only lets the page read them.

Where is my scan? Step by step
  1. Put the hospital's CD or DVD in this computer. A phone cannot read a disc.
  2. Press Choose the disc or a folder, above.
  3. In the window that opens, click the disc. It is often called D: or E:, or carries the hospital's name. Then press the button at the bottom of that window.
  4. Your browser will ask whether this page may read the files. Say yes. They stay on this computer.

Got a ZIP from a patient portal instead? Press Choose a ZIP file and pick it from your downloads.

To check, turn off Wi-Fi and open your scan. It still works, because the scan is read on this device.

Anatomy outlines from your own computer

If you ran rAidiology's CT outliner on your own computer, it saved a small file ending in .raidiology.json.gz. Add it here to see the organs and bones it outlined, named on your own pictures. It shows anatomy only: which part is which, never whether anything is wrong.

The file holds no name, patient number or date of birth. This tab reads it, and it goes nowhere.
