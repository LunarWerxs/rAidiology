Where's my scan? Here's how to find it
  1. Put the hospital's CD or DVD in this computer. A phone can't read a disc.
  2. Press Choose the disc or a folder, above.
  3. In the window that opens, click the disc. It's often called D: or E:, or has the hospital's name. Then press the button at the bottom of that window.
  4. Your browser will ask whether this page may read the files. Say yes. They stay on this computer.

Got a ZIP from a patient portal instead? Press Choose a ZIP file and pick it from your downloads.

To check, turn off Wi-Fi and open your scan. It still works, because your browser reads it on this device.
