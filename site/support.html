What the money buys

Training runs today on one home graphics card. Rented data-centre GPUs train bigger models faster. RunPod's listed prices in October 2026: an A100 80GB GPU is $1.59 an hour and an H100 SXM is $3.49 an hour.

So $5 is about 3 hours of A100 time, $15 about 9 hours and $50 about 31 hours.
