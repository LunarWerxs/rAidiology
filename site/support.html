Does paying add anything to the app?

No. Everyone gets the same free app. The perks live on Discord, in the supporters list and on the project's GitHub page, and they never include early or private access to a research model.

Does company sponsorship cover commercial use?

No. Using the models in a product or an internal tool needs a commercial license, and a sponsorship doesn't include one.
