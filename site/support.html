No. LunarWerx LLC is a for-profit company, so payments are not tax-deductible.

No. The app stays free for everyone. The perks live on Discord, the supporters list and the README.

Why does my card statement say LINK.COM?

Connections Global LLC sells the licences as LunarWerx LLC's authorised reseller. Link, from Stripe, is the merchant of record: it takes the payment and handles sales tax, refunds and disputes. Your card statement starts with LINK.COM.

Does company sponsorship cover commercial use?

No. Sponsorship is support, not a licence. Using the models in a product or an internal tool needs a commercial licence.
