See or delete your donation

Open, save or delete a scan you donated. You won't need an account or a name.

With your number

Open your donation

Type the number you were shown when you donated. Your browser fetches the locked files from our storage and unlocks them here, on this device.

Without it

Lost your number?

Open the same scan you donated. Your browser makes a short code from its pictures, on this device. Email us that code and we can find the donation and delete it. Nothing is sent from this page.

    What your number lets you do

    There's no account and no name on file, so your number is how we know the donation is yours.

    • Open a copy

      Look through the pictures you sent, here in your browser.

    • Save a copy

      Download the DICOM files as a ZIP, exactly as they were sent.

    • Save the permission

      Keep a copy of the permission you gave when you donated.

    • Delete it

      This removes the scan and withdraws your permission. After that, the number stops working.
